About this Privacy Policy

Swap is an app that allows airline crew members to list, request, and find duty swaps.

This Privacy Policy explains how RPS Studio AB ("we", "us", or "our") processes personal data when you use the Swap mobile application ("Swap" or the "App").

Swap is an independent service. It is not operated by, affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to your airline. We do not provide your imported roster, personal information, or swap activity to your airline.

2

Data Controller

RPS Studio AB

Privacy enquiries: hej@rpsstudio.se

3

Personal Data Processed by Swap

Depending on how you use Swap, the App may process the following information:

  • Profile and contact information, such as your name, email address, telephone number, airline or operator, crew category, base, qualification, and other information you choose to add.
  • Roster and calendar information, such as duty dates, duty times, flight numbers, airports, destinations, sectors, duty types, standby periods, days off, and other roster-related details you choose to import or enter.
  • Marketplace information, such as listings, swap requests, offers, responses, and contact-sharing status.
4

Your Private Roster

Your complete imported roster is private.

Your private roster is stored using Apple CloudKit in your private iCloud database. This means it is associated with your iCloud account and synchronised securely through Apple's cloud infrastructure.

Your private roster is not publicly visible to other Swap users. It is not provided to your airline. It is not visible to us through the CloudKit developer portal.

Importing a roster does not automatically publish your duties. A duty becomes visible in the marketplace only when you actively choose to create a listing.

5

Marketplace Listings

When you actively publish a listing, Swap makes a limited amount of information available to other Swap users so that they can understand and respond to the listing.

A listing may include information such as:

  • Duty date and time
  • Duty type
  • Flight number
  • Airports, destinations, or sectors
  • Whether you want to exchange or give away the duty
  • Other limited information required to describe the listing

Your complete roster is never published.

Your name, email address, and telephone number are not displayed publicly in the marketplace.

6

Contact Details After Contact

Your contact details are disclosed only to the other participating user when:

  • You tap Contact on another user's offer;
  • Another user taps Contact on your offer; or
  • Both users otherwise choose to share contact details through the App.

This allows the participating users to contact each other and complete any required steps outside Swap, such as using an airline's official systems and procedures.

Your contact details are not disclosed to users who are merely browsing listings.

7

How CloudKit Is Used

Swap uses Apple CloudKit, an Apple-operated cloud-storage service built on iCloud infrastructure.

In practical terms, CloudKit allows the App to store and synchronise information securely using Apple's cloud servers.

Swap separates information according to its purpose:

  • Your complete roster and private profile data are stored privately and are associated with your iCloud account.
  • Marketplace listings contain only the limited information required for other users to understand and respond to a listing.
  • Contact information is disclosed only after a user chooses to contact the other participating crew member.

Apple processes data as the provider of the underlying cloud infrastructure. Apple's processing is also governed by Apple's applicable privacy information and iCloud terms.

8

How We Use Personal Data

Personal data is processed only where necessary to:

  • Create and maintain your Swap profile
  • Import, display, and manage your roster within the App
  • Allow you to publish duty-swap listings
  • Display relevant listings to other Swap users
  • Allow users to make, receive, and respond to offers
  • Allow participating users to contact each other after choosing Contact
  • Provide support and respond to questions
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the App
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements
  • Establish, exercise, or defend legal claims where necessary

We do not sell your personal data.

We do not use your personal data for advertising.

We do not use your personal data for cross-app tracking.

9

Legal Basis for Processing

Where the General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR") or equivalent data-protection rules apply, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract: We process information required to provide the functions you request, including profile management, roster import, marketplace listings, offers, and contact-sharing records.
  • Consent: Where required, we ask for your permission before accessing information such as calendar data. You can withdraw device permissions at any time through your device settings.
  • Legitimate interests: Where applicable, we may process limited information required to operate, secure, and troubleshoot Swap, provided that these interests do not override your rights and freedoms.
  • Legal obligations and legal claims: We may process information where required by applicable law or where necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.
10

Calendar Access

Swap requests access to your calendar only when required for a function you choose to use and only after you grant permission on your device.

Calendar access is used only to import, display, and manage roster-related information within Swap.

You can withdraw calendar access at any time in your device settings. Withdrawing permission prevents Swap from reading additional calendar information. It does not automatically remove information that you previously imported into the App.

11

When Data Is Shared

We do not provide your personal data, private roster, or swap activity to your airline.

We do not sell your personal data to advertisers, data brokers, or other businesses.

Information is disclosed only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Other Swap users may see the limited duty information contained in a listing you actively publish.
  • The other participating user receives your relevant contact details after one of you chooses Contact.
  • Apple processes information through CloudKit as the provider of the underlying cloud infrastructure.
  • Information may be disclosed where required by applicable law, a binding legal process, or a valid request from a competent authority.
12

Official Airline Procedures

Swap is a marketplace for finding and agreeing potential duty swaps between users.

Swap does not replace your airline's official systems, approvals, procedures, contractual requirements, or applicable flight-time limitations.

An agreement reached through Swap does not mean that your airline has approved the swap.

You remain responsible for completing any required airline procedure and ensuring that a proposed swap is permitted.

13

Data Retention

Personal data is retained only for as long as necessary to provide Swap and fulfil the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

In general:

  • Your profile and private roster information are retained while your account remains active.
  • Active listings are retained while they remain available in the marketplace.
  • Withdrawn, rejected, expired, or completed listings are deleted or retained only for the limited period required to provide relevant history features, resolve disputes, or comply with applicable law.
  • Contact details shared with another user may remain visible to that participating user as part of the contact-sharing record.

When you delete your account, personal data associated with the account is deleted or anonymised unless retention is required by law or strictly necessary to resolve an active dispute.

14

Account Deletion and Permissions

You can request deletion of your Swap account and associated personal data in the App:

Settings > Account > Delete Account

You can also contact us at hej@rpsstudio.se.

You can withdraw calendar access at any time through your device settings.

To remove previously imported roster information without deleting your account, use the relevant deletion option in the App.

16

Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal data against unauthorised access, loss, alteration, or disclosure.

Private roster information is stored using your private CloudKit database. Marketplace listings contain only the limited information required for the marketplace to function.

However, no system can guarantee absolute security. You should protect access to your device and Apple account and avoid publishing information that is not required for a proposed duty swap.
